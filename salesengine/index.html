A better way to sell

Sales is a skill. Build it on purpose.

Selling well isn't about pushing harder. It's about understanding people, asking better questions and making the next step clear. The Sales Engine is online sales training designed to help you practise those skills in a supportive, structured setting.

Across two weeks, you'll work through the sales conversation from preparation to follow-up. Expect useful frameworks, practice you can put to work immediately and thoughtful feedback from your tutors.

Eight sessions. One complete sales process.

90 minutes each. Live. Practical.

Session 1 | Mon, Nov 23: Sales Fundamentals and the Modern Buyer

How buyers think, what value really means, and how the sales cycle works.

You produce: a one-page sales brief.

Lead: Bada Muhammad Aluthaimeen

Session 2 | Tue, Nov 24: Lead Generation and Prospect Qualification

Find the right people and decide who is worth your time.

You produce: a researched 20-prospect list.

Lead: Funmilayo Tumule

Session 3 | Thu, Nov 26: Cold Outreach and Cold Calling

Reach strangers with relevance, not noise.

You produce: a call script, an email and a direct message.

Lead: Bada Muhammad Aluthaimeen

Session 4 | Sat, Nov 28: Buyer Psychology and Ethical Persuasion

Understand decisions. Influence without pressure or deception.

You produce: three buyer diagnoses with ethical responses.

Lead: Bada Muhammad Aluthaimeen

Session 5 | Mon, Nov 30: Discovery and Needs Analysis

Ask better questions. Diagnose before you pitch.

You produce: a 10-question discovery guide.

Lead: Funmilayo Tumule

Session 6 | Tue, Dec 1: Value Propositions and Objection Handling

Say why it matters, and answer “no” with confidence.

You produce: live objection responses.

Lead: Bada Muhammad Aluthaimeen

Session 7 | Thu, Dec 3: Negotiation, Closing and Follow-Up

Reach clear decisions and keep every deal moving.

You produce: a negotiated, recorded deal.

Lead: Bada Muhammad Aluthaimeen

Session 8 | Sat, Dec 5: Final Sales Simulation and Assessment

The full sales challenge, from customer definition to agreed next step.

You produce: your final sales portfolio.

Co-leads: Bada Muhammad Aluthaimeen and Funmilayo Tumule
